8. **This week:** read your contract, order a mic, pick a name and install QGIS. This takes about 3 hours in total.

**Plan window:** 13 Oct 2026 – 17 Jan 2027
**Written for:** a skilled video editor and UPSC aspirant who works 4 days × 4 hours a week with Ayush's team

> **How to use this plan.** Read it fully once. After that, open only three parts each week: the box above, the **timetable** (Section 3.2) and **this month's checklist** (Section 5). Use the other sections only to look things up. Numbers marked *(est.)* are estimates, not promises.

**Words used in this plan**
- **RPM:** the money *you* receive for every 1,000 views, after YouTube takes its share.
- **YPP (YouTube Partner Program):** the program you must join before YouTube pays you anything.
- **Watch hours:** the total hours people spend watching your *long* videos. Shorts do not count.
- **CTR (click-through rate):** the % of people who saw your thumbnail and clicked on it. For a new channel, 3–5% is normal and 5% or more is good.
- **PYQ:** a previous year question from the UPSC exam.
- **Mid-roll:** an ad in the middle of a video. Only videos of 8+ minutes can have one.
- **Affiliate link:** a link that pays you a small cut when someone buys through it.
- **Retainer:** a fixed monthly fee a client pays you for regular work.

## 2. Positioning: who you are on YouTube

**Niche:** map-first geopolitics explainers for Hindi-speaking UPSC aspirants and curious young Indians. Every video answers one question: **"India pe asar kya hai?"**

**Language:** Hindi-first Hinglish.
- Speak conversational Hindi, but keep English terms like "strategic autonomy", "chokepoint" and "Global South".
- Use English labels on maps.
- Add English subtitles from day 1.
- Test titles and thumbnails with YouTube's **Test & Compare** tool. It works on desktop only and needs "advanced features" turned on. Use **2 versions**, and YouTube picks the winner by watch time.

**Why Hindi:**
- The biggest UPSC IR channels (StudyIQ, Drishti, Unacademy World Affairs) are in Hindi or both languages.
- English pays more per view, but you would be competing with WION, Firstpost and big global channels.
- Your voice clone will also sound most natural in Hindi.

**5 name ideas.** Check that both handles are free, and that nobody already uses the name as a brand.

| Name | Meaning and feel |
|---|---|
| **Naksha Nazar** | "Map view". Short, easy to remember, and shows your map style |
| **GeoSutra** | "The thread or formula of geopolitics". Works in Hindi and English |
| **Duniya Map Par** | "The world on a map". Says exactly what you do |
| **Nakshe Ki Baat** | "Talk of the map". Friendly and conversational |
| **Sarhad Se Samandar** | "From border to ocean". Covers land and sea |

Avoid names that use "Chanakya", "Kootneeti", "Gravitas" or "Vantage", because these are already brands. Put your **real name** in the About section, for example: "by [Your Name], UPSC aspirant and video editor".

**Your edge: four things that no single channel combines today**
1. **UPSC-grade accuracy.** Every claim is checked against primary sources: MEA, PIB, UN, PRS.
2. **Editor-level visuals.** Animated maps, timelines and data callouts. Most Hindi IR channels use a smart-board or a talking head.
3. **The UPSC corner.** The last 60–90 seconds of every video give the syllabus code, one Mains question, 5 keywords, the places to know for Prelims, and the linked PYQ.
4. **A calm tone.** Most Hindi defence content is loud. You are the calm voice that shows its sources.

**Video structure (use it every time)**
1. Open on a moving map (0–20 seconds): the conflict, the stakes and one question.
2. Your reusable **8–10 second on-camera intro**, in your real voice (Section 5, Week 1).
3. "India pe asar kya?" within the first 60 seconds.
4. Three acts: history → geography → now.
5. "What next": 2–3 possible scenarios.
6. The **UPSC corner (60–90 seconds), recorded on camera in your real voice**.

**Length:**
- Videos 1–3: 8–10 minutes, while you learn the pipeline.
- From Video 4: 10–15 minutes. Videos of 8+ minutes can carry mid-rolls later.
- The year-recap video on 2 Jan: 15–18 minutes.

**Shorts: same channel, fewer on YouTube.**
- Make **2 Shorts a week**, cut from that week's long video.
- Post **both on Instagram**. Post **one on YouTube**: the first one in that week's list in Section 6.2.
- **Why only one on YouTube:** a study of 18,000 channels found the best growth when Shorts were 25–40% of uploads. Above about 55%, the audience for long videos got smaller. Every YouTube money tier depends on long-video watch hours.
- At a monthly review you may start posting both on YouTube, but only if each long video's views are not falling.
- **A separate Shorts channel:** not now. Look at the question again at the decision point in Week 13.

**Thumbnail formula:** one map + one red arrow or highlight + one face or flag + 2–4 words. Use faces only from the safe sources in Section 10.

**Title rules:**
- 40–60 characters, with the main keyword first.
- The title and thumbnail must match what the video delivers. YouTube enforces this strictly for news videos in India.
- **Status-word rule:** some titles contain a "status word" such as band, signed, khatam or toot raha. Within 24 hours before publishing, check that word against a primary source.

## 4. The production system

### 4.1 The pipeline: one long video + 2 Shorts a week

**Main rule: "fully automated" is NOT the goal.**
- YouTube does not pay channels that look mass-produced or just read out news text.
- Since July 2026, YouTube also refuses to pay channels that use an AI "persona" presented as a politics expert.
- Claude and ElevenLabs save you time. **Your judgment, your analysis and your editing make the channel original, and safe to monetize.**

| # | Step | Who does it | Hours: first 3 videos → from Video 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm the topic (already fixed in Section 6.1) and get a source brief | Claude finds primary sources (MEA, PIB, UN, PRS, World Bank) | 0.25 → 0.25 |
| 2 | Read the sources | **You.** This counts as UPSC study | 2 → 1.5 |
| 3 | Main argument | **You**, before asking Claude for anything. Write one paragraph: what is *your* argument? | 0.5 → 0.5 |
| 4 | First draft | Claude writes it from your outline (~1,300–1,500 words for 10 minutes) | 0.5 → 0.5 |
| 5 | Rewrite | **You.** Add at least 3 points of your own analysis. Mark them [MINE] in the draft and keep them in the final script | 1.5 → 1.5 |
| 6 | Fact-check | Claude lists every claim in a table → **you check each one at its source** | 1.5 → 1 |
| 7 | Voice-over (video + 2 Short scripts) | ElevenLabs reads it in your clone voice → **you fix names and pronunciation** | 1 → 0.75 |
| 8 | UPSC corner | **You, on camera, in your real voice** | 0.33 → 0.33 |
| 9 | Maps and visuals | **You.** India_master + base maps, PIB/DD, Pexels | 3 → 2 |
| 10 | Edit | **You**, in Premiere or DaVinci Resolve | 7 → 5 |
| 11 | English subtitles | Auto-captions → **you correct them** | 0.5 → 0.5 |
| 12 | Title, 2 thumbnails, description, chapters | Claude gives 10–15 titles and thumbnail ideas → **you design** | 1.5 → 1 |
| 13 | Upload, schedule, AI label, Test & Compare | You | 0.5 → 0.25 |
| 14 | 2 Shorts | Claude writes hook scripts (120–150 words) → **you cut them** from the same maps → you schedule them on YouTube and Instagram | 3 → 1.75 |
| 15 | UPSC note page | Claude drafts it from the final script → you check it | 0.75 → 0.75 |
| 16 | Comments | You | 0.5 → 0.5 |
| | **Total** | | **~24 → ~18** |

- The first videos take about 24 hours, which does not fit in 19. That is why Month 1 has only 2 videos and one week with no video, and why Videos 1–3 are shorter.
- From Video 4, about 18 hours fits inside your 19 hours, with a small buffer.
- **The split:** Claude does about 30–40% of the work (research, drafts, titles, notes). You do the 60–70% that makes the channel yours.

### 4.2 Set up a Claude Project once

Claude Free allows up to 5 Projects. Create one called "Channel" and paste in these standing instructions:

```
I make Hindi-first Hinglish geopolitics explainers for UPSC aspirants.
1. Use primary sources first (MEA, PIB, UN, PRS, World Bank/IMF, treaty texts). Always give links.
2. Never invent a quote, number or date. If unsure, write [CHECK].
3. List every factual claim in a table: claim | source link | primary? | verified (I fill).
4. Tag every topic to the UPSC syllabus (GS2 IR a-d, GS3, GS1, Prelims places).
5. Draft only from my outline and main argument. Offer analysis as options;
   I write the final opinions myself and mark them [MINE].
6. Keep a calm, non-sensational tone. No graphic descriptions of war.
7. No "sources say" claims about Indian military operations. Use only official MEA/MoD/PIB briefings.
8. After the final script: 2 Short scripts (120-150 words, hook first), 1 note page,
   5 MCQs and 1 Mains question.
```

**What Claude does for you each week:**
- The source brief and the script draft.
- The claims table and title ideas.
- The Shorts scripts, the note page and the Mains question.
- In Weeks 8–9, personalised pitch emails.

Use **NotebookLM Free** alongside Claude when you want answers based only on PDFs you upload, such as MEA briefs, PRS notes or UN reports.

### 4.3 Steps that always stay human

- The final topic and angle.
- Every opinion and every judgment.
- Signing off every fact against a primary source. In a 2025 EBU/BBC study, 45% of AI assistant answers about news had a significant error.
- Checking India's borders on every map, thumbnail and Short.
- Tone and wording on sensitive topics: India–Pakistan, India–China, religion.
- Any criticism of a named person or organisation, because of the risk of a defamation case.
- Making sure every title and thumbnail is honest.

### 4.4 Tools in the pipeline

- **Editing:** Premiere (your existing skill) or DaVinci Resolve Free. Never use your employer's software licence.
- **Shorts:** Instagram's free **Edits** app. It adds no watermark and makes automatic captions in one or two languages. CapCut is banned in India.
  - Keep something moving on screen every 3–5 seconds. Meta does not pay for static videos, image slideshows or text-only videos.
- **Maps:** your checked **India_master** file and 6 base maps (Section 5, Week 1). Animate them with your own keyframes in Premiere or After Effects.
  - For close-ups under 300 km, see the map rule in Section 10.
  - **Skip Google Earth Studio.** Its FAQ says Google does not license Earth imagery for commercial use, and its borders do not follow the Survey of India.
  - For satellite views, use Copernicus Sentinel-2 (credit: "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2026") or NASA Worldview (credit: NASA).
  - Skip paid plugins like GeoLayers for now.
- **Footage:**
  - PIB, Doordarshan and Prasar Bharati: free for creators since May 2025. The conditions are in Section 10.
  - DVIDS (US military footage): add its required disclaimer.
  - Pexels and Pixabay.
- **Music:** YouTube Audio Library only. **Use no music in Shorts.**
- **Scheduling:** YouTube Studio and Meta Business Suite (both free).

## 11. When you start overthinking

1. **Decide once.** The week's topic is already fixed in Section 6.1. Confirm it on Monday at 15:30, in 5 minutes. Only the breaking-news rule can change it.
2. **Use a "good enough" checklist, then publish.** Once the facts are checked, the map borders are correct, the audio is clean and the title is honest, you publish. Any extra improvement goes into the next video. Make at most 2 thumbnail versions and let Test & Compare decide.
3. **Look at numbers only at the monthly review.** When you feel the urge to check stats, open the next task in the pipeline instead. One video's views tell you almost nothing; eleven videos tell you a lot.
4. **Write it down, then work for 25 minutes.** When worries pile up (money, family, UPSC, the future), write them on paper for 5 minutes. Then pick the smallest next task and work on it for 25 minutes. Doing something reduces overthinking; more thinking does not.

Your family needs you healthy more than they need a fast-growing channel. If stress starts hurting your sleep or health, talk to a doctor or counsellor. India's free mental-health helpline, **Tele-MANAS**, is **14416**. It is open 24×7 and works in many languages. Asking for help is a strong move, not a weak one.

**Remember:** an empire is built from boring, repeated weeks. Your job for the next 13 weeks is simple: **one honest video a week, two Shorts, one UPSC page, and enough sleep.**

- **Plan with the ₹20–35 rows.** ₹50–100 needs exam-prep advertisers to bid on your videos, and nobody has shown that they do for this niche yet.
- Some conflict videos may get limited or no ads while YouTube treats the event as sensitive.
- Channels that get 7–30 lakh views a month usually have tens of thousands to 1 lakh+ subscribers and 50–100+ videos. By January you will have 11 videos.

### A.2 ₹50,000 a month from Shorts and Instagram

**YouTube Shorts pay very little in India.** Expect about ₹1–₹5 per 1,000 engaged views, maybe up to ₹10 (est.). An engaged view is one where the viewer stayed past the first seconds. One Indian creator reported earning about ₹0.9 per 1,000.

| Shorts RPM | Views needed per month | Views needed per day |
|---|---|---|
| ₹1 | 5 crore | 16.7 lakh |
| ₹2 | 2.5 crore | 8.3 lakh |
| ₹5 | 1 crore | 3.3 lakh |
| ₹10 | 50 lakh | 1.7 lakh |

- All your Shorts together will probably get under 5 lakh views in 3 months. That is less than what a single day of the ₹50k target needs.
- Since Aug 2026, the public view count includes every play, but only *engaged* views pay. So the number you see will be higher than the number that earns.

**Instagram pays ₹0 per view in India.** There is no Reels revenue share, and the Reels bonus is closed to Indian creators. Money on Instagram comes only from brand deals, affiliate links and small "Gifts".
- ₹50k a month needs about **25,000–50,000 engaged followers**. That takes 9–18 months if growth goes well (est.).
- At the typical price of ₹4,750 per Reel, even 6–10 deals a month make only ₹28k–48k. That many deals would also turn half your feed into ads.
- A realistic ₹50k mix at that size:
  - 3–4 deals a month at ₹6–10k each (₹18–40k), each sold as a Reel + YouTube Short package.
  - Plus affiliate and PDF income (₹5–10k).

### A.3 The YouTube rule change on 1 Feb 2027

| | Until 31 Jan 2027 | From 1 Feb 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Join YPP for ad money | 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours in 12 months, or 10M Shorts views in 90 days | 1,000 subs + **8,000** watch hours, or **20M** Shorts views |
| Earn from Shorts | You must already be in the YPP ads tier; no extra view minimum | You also need 10M Shorts views every 90 days |
| Fan-funding tier (memberships, Super Thanks, Shopping) | 500 subs + 3,000 watch hours, or 3M Shorts views | **Unchanged** |

At 5 minutes of average watch time, 4,000 hours is about 48,000 views, and 8,000 hours is about 1 lakh views. Only public long videos count; Shorts watch time does not.
