# Daily Log

One line per work session, in this format:

    YYYY-MM-DD | goal-id | hours | what you actually did

Rules that keep this honest:

- Log the same day. Backfilling more than 24h is guessing, and guesses poison the pace math.
- `hours` means focused hours only, not "sat at the desk" hours.
- Did nothing? Log a zero with the reason. A zero is data; a missing day is a hole.
- Entries go below the divider line. Everything above it is ignored by the tracker.
